The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The developer does not collect, store, sell or share any of your personal data.
  • Your location is used on your iPhone to find the stops nearest to you. It is never sent to the developer.

Your location

With your permission (“While Using the App” only), the app reads your location to find the nearest train stations, bus stops and ferry wharves, show which way you are facing, and estimate walking distance. This happens on your iPhone.

To work out walking time and draw the walking route, the app asks Apple Maps for directions from your location to the stop. Apple handles that request under the Apple Privacy Policy.

If you tap “Google Maps”, the app opens Google Maps with the stop’s coordinates. Google Maps then follows the Google Privacy Policy.

You can say no to location access. The app then offers a city-centre demo instead. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Network requests

The app connects to two services. Neither request contains your location or any personal information:

  • Auckland Transport realtime API: to get live arrival times and delays. The app sends only the trip numbers of nearby services.
  • GitHub Pages: to download timetable updates published from Auckland Transport’s open data.

Like any website, these services can see standard connection information such as your IP address. The developer has no access to it.

Stored on your iPhone

The app keeps your transport-type settings (train, bus, ferry) and the latest downloaded timetable on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

你的位置

經你同意（僅限「使用 App 期間」）後，App 會讀取你的位置，用來找出最近的火車站、巴士站與渡輪碼頭、顯示你面向的方向，並估算步行距離。這些都在你的 iPhone 上進行。

為了計算步行時間並畫出步行路線，App 會向 Apple 地圖查詢從你的位置到車站的路線，由 Apple 依 Apple 隱私權政策處理。

如果你點「Google 地圖」，App 會帶著車站座標打開 Google 地圖，之後依 Google 隱私權政策處理。

你可以不給定位權限，App 會改用市中心示範位置。之後隨時可以到「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 定位服務」修改。

網路連線

App 只會連到以下兩個服務，傳送的內容都不含你的位置或任何個人資料：

  • Auckland Transport 即時資料：取得即時到站時間與誤點。App 只送出附近班次的班次編號。
  • GitHub Pages：下載由 Auckland Transport 開放資料產生的時刻表更新。

和瀏覽任何網站一樣，這些服務會看到一般連線資訊（例如 IP 位址），開發者無法取得這些資訊。

存在你 iPhone 上的資料

App 會在你的 iPhone 上保存交通工具的開關設定（火車、巴士、渡輪）以及最新下載的時刻表。刪除 App 即全部清除。
